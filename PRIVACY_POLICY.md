# Privacy Policy for Digital Photo Frame

**Effective Date: August 11, 2026**

Amazing Apps ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Digital Photo Frame mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We use your data to provide and improve the App. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

### 1.1 Types of Data Collected

#### Personal Data
While using our App, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"). Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:
*   Usage Data (via Google Play Services)
*   Location Data (for weather information)

#### Media and Files
The App requires access to your device's photo gallery to display images. 
*   **Local Processing:** Photos are accessed locally on your device. We **do not** upload, store, or share your photos on our servers or with any third parties.

#### Location Data
We may use and store information about your location if you give us permission to do so ("Location Data"). We use this data to provide features of our App, specifically to fetch local weather information.
*   You can enable or disable location services at any time through your device settings.

## 2. Use of Data
Digital Photo Frame uses the collected data for various purposes:
*   To provide and maintain the App
*   To notify you about changes to our App
*   To provide customer care and support
*   To provide local weather information based on your current location

## 3. Service Providers
We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our App ("Service Providers"), to provide the App on our behalf, or to perform App-related services. These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

*   **Google Play Services:** Used for app functionality and analytics.
*   **OpenWeatherMap API:** Used to fetch weather data based on your location coordinates.

## 4. Security of Data
The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.
